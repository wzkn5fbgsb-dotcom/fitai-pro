# FITAI PRO Health Notice

## Not medical advice
FITAI PRO provides **general guidance** on training and nutrition for **healthy adults**. Routines, calories, macronutrients, meal plans and coach answers (rules or artificial intelligence) are **estimates**. They **do not replace** diagnosis, treatment or advice from a physician, registered dietitian or other healthcare professional. FITAI PRO is not a medical device.

## Talk to a professional before starting if you:
- have heart disease, high blood pressure, diabetes, kidney or liver disease, or another medical condition;
- take medications that affect appetite, weight, heart rate or blood glucose;
- are pregnant, postpartum or breastfeeding;
- have or have had an eating disorder;
- have injuries, persistent pain or recent surgery;
- have not exercised in a long time or are over 65.

The app asks safety questions and, in some cases, **does not show calorie targets or diets** or **does not generate a routine**. This is a precaution, not a diagnosis.

## While exercising
- **Stop immediately** if you feel pain (especially in the chest, arm, jaw or joints), dizziness, unusual shortness of breath, palpitations, nausea or blurred vision.
- Use correct technique and loads you can control. Warm up before training.
- If pain or symptoms persist, seek medical care.

## Nutrition
- Food values come from USDA FoodData Central and may vary by brand, preparation and actual portion size.
- Always check labels if you have **allergies**: the app filters allergens but cannot guarantee a specific product is free of traces.
- Do not follow very restrictive diets. The app limits deficits, but you know your body: if you feel extreme hunger, fatigue or dizziness, talk to a professional.

## Help and resources (United States)
- **Medical emergency:** call **911**.
- **Emotional crisis or thoughts of harming yourself:** call or text **988** (988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, 24/7, English and Spanish).
- **Eating disorders:** **National Alliance for Eating Disorders**, **1-866-662-1235**.

Outside the US, contact your country's emergency services.

## Results
Results vary from person to person and depend on many factors. We do not guarantee any specific weight, body composition or performance outcome.
