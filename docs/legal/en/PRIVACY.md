FITAI PRO is a training and nutrition app for **adults (18+)**, primarily for users in the United States. This policy explains what data we collect, why, who we share it with, and your rights.

## 1. Summary
- We collect only what is needed to create and adjust your plan.
- Your health, body and photo data are **sensitive data**: we process them with your **consent**, they are private, and only you can access them in the app.
- We **do not sell** your data, **do not** share it for advertising, and **do not** use cross-app ad tracking.
- We **do not** use your photos to train artificial intelligence models.
- You can **delete your account and all your data** in the app at any time.

## 2. Data we collect
| Category | Examples | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Account | email, password (hashed by our auth provider), user ID | you |
| Body and health data | age, sex, height, weight, measurements, goal, activity level, injuries or discomfort, answers to safety questions (e.g., pregnancy, history of eating disorders, medical conditions) | you |
| Activity and nutrition | routines, sets, weights, reps, perceived effort, pain, meals logged, weekly check-ins | you |

We do not collect precise location, contacts, microphone, or advertising identifiers.

If you use the app **without an account (local mode)**, your data stays only on your phone and we do not receive it.

## 6. Retention and deletion
- We keep your data while you have an account.
- **Delete account** (Profile → Delete account): we delete your account, profile, logs, measurements, check-ins, messages and photos from storage, immediately in our database. Provider backups are overwritten within up to [30] days [VERIFY].
- Payment records may be kept, de-linked from your account, as long as tax law requires.
- Deleting your account **does not cancel** an Apple or Google subscription: cancel it in your store settings.
- You can delete individual photos at any time.

## 7. Security
Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), per-user access control in the database, private photo storage with temporary links, secret keys only on the server, and error logs without personal data. No system is 100% secure; if a breach affects you, we will notify you as required by law.
